Run&Gui Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal data we process in the Run&Gui application (the "App"), for what purpose, on what legal basis, and what rights you have. Please read it carefully: the App processes health data, which the GDPR treats as a special category and which we protect accordingly.

For any question about your data or to exercise your rights, write to that address.

2. What data we process

We collect only what is necessary to prepare your training plan and follow up on it:

a) Identification and contact data Full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp.

b) Physical and sports data Age, sex, weight, height, goal, experience, weekly availability, paces, heart-rate/watch data, and the training plan your coach prepares for you.

c) Health data (special category, Art. 9 GDPR)

  • The PAR-Q physical readiness questionnaire, injuries, and other health matters you declare.
  • If you give your optional consent to menstrual cycle tracking: hormonal contraceptive use, regularity, cycle and bleeding length, date of your last period, heavy bleeding, symptoms and notes; as well as the daily cycle entries you choose to record.

d) Communications The messages you exchange with your coach inside the App and the messages you write in the App's chats (the one that helps you fill in your profile and the one that adapts your plan). Because you write those messages as free text, they may contain information about your health; section 3.1 explains what happens to them and where they go.

e) Technical and subscription data Your account identifier, your device's push notification token, and your subscription status (active/expired). Payment is handled by Apple: we do not process or store your card details.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not build profiles from your health data.

3.1 What exactly is sent to the AI assistant

We would rather list what is sent than promise you what is not. A promise about what never leaves can break without anyone noticing the day we add a feature; a list of what does leave forces us to update this policy when it changes.

When the AI assistant is involved (generating your plan, adapting it from the chat, preparing your coach's draft reply, or the chat that helps you fill in your profile), the following is sent to the model provider (Anthropic, see section 5), and nothing else:

1. Your training profile: profile version, age, sex, weight, height, experience, goal, weekly availability, preferences, strength, reference paces and heart rates, logistics and, if you have given your explicit consent for health data, also your PAR-Q questionnaire, the injuries and health notes you have declared and, if you consented to its tracking, your menstrual cycle data. The assistant uses them to adapt your plan with all the safety information in front of it (for example, not proposing intervals while your questionnaire has an unresolved yes).

2. Your training plan: the overall structure and the weeks with their sessions.

3. Your recent runs recorded by your watch: date, distance, duration and pace.

4. Signals computed on your own device from Apple Health or Health Connect data: fatigue, kilometres in the last week, acute versus chronic load, load ramp, weekly readiness and load relative to plan; and, if you have them, your VO2max and your estimated paces. The rest of the raw watch data (beat-to-beat heart rate, sleep, full activities) is not sent: it stays on your device and only the computed signals travel.

5. The adjustments you make on the Adapt screen: minutes available per day and relative intensity.

6. The text you write, exactly as you write it: your messages in the Adapt chat along with the most recent messages in that same conversation, your messages in the chat that helps you fill in your profile, and the most recent messages you exchange with your coach (so their draft reply has context).

Read point 6 carefully, because it is the one that affects you most. That text is sent unfiltered. If you describe an ache, pain or injury in it, that information about your health is sent to the model provider. This is not a side effect: the system is designed to read it. If you describe pain, the assistant identifies it and the engine uses it to ease, hold back or stop the relevant sessions, which is precisely what makes it safe to adapt a plan in writing.

If you would rather not send free text, you can use the Controls mode on the Adapt screen (minutes and intensity, no free text). Bear in mind that, with your health consent, your PAR-Q questionnaire and declared health notes also travel to the assistant (item 1 of this list): if you withdraw that consent, they stop travelling and your plan is adapted without that safety information.

What is never sent: your name, your email address, your phone or WhatsApp number, and the raw activities from your watch. Those fields are excluded by a closed list in the code itself, checked automatically on every change.

4. Consent for health data

Processing your health data requires your explicit consent, which we collect separately and granularly in the App (PAR-Q and menstrual cycle separately) before asking you for that data. That consent also covers sending that data to the AI assistant's provider (Anthropic, sections 3.1 and 6), which is part of how your plan is adapted safely. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or writing to us; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing. Without consent for health data it is not possible to prepare the plan safely.

If you created your account before the date of this version of the policy, this sending to the AI assistant is a change with respect to what you consented to: it will not apply to your account until you expressly accept it inside the App.

5. Recipients and data processors

To provide the service we rely on providers that process data on our behalf, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Supabase (hosting and database). Your data, including health data, is stored on servers in the European Union, encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Apple (App distribution, subscription payment management, and push notification delivery on iOS).
  • RevenueCat (technical management of subscription status). It receives an identifier of your account and subscription data; it does not receive your health data.
  • Expo (push notification delivery service). It processes your device token; it does not receive your health data.
  • Anthropic (AI assistant that generates and adapts the plan). It receives exactly what section 3.1 lists: your training profile (including, with your health consent, the PAR-Q, your health notes and your cycle data), your plan, your runs from your watch, the signals computed on your device (including your VO2max if you have it), the adjustments from the Adapt screen and the text you write in the App's chats. It does not receive your name, email or phone number.

Your coach accesses your profile, your plan and your messages to provide the service.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

6. International transfers

Where your data is stored: everything we store, including your health data (PAR-Q, injuries and menstrual cycle), is kept on servers in the European Union (Supabase, EU region). It is not moved anywhere else for storage.

What is processed outside the European Union: some technical providers process data in the United States, and you should know exactly which:

  • RevenueCat, Expo and Apple: account identifier, your device token and subscription status. None of them receives health data.
  • Anthropic: everything listed in section 3.1. That includes the text you write in the App's chats and, with your health consent, your PAR-Q questionnaire, your declared health notes and your cycle data. This is a transfer of special category data and we make it on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9.2.a).

These transfers rely on GDPR safeguards (the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), in addition to the data processing agreement we hold with each provider.

If you would rather your health information did not leave the European Union, you can decline (or withdraw) the health consent: the App keeps working with Controls mode and without that safety information in front of the assistant.

7. Retention period

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account (see section 8) or withdraw your consent, we delete your data, including health data. We only keep the minimum information the law requires us to retain (for example, records related to tax or commercial obligations) for the legally required periods, blocked for any other use.

8. Your rights

You can exercise at any time your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability and to withdraw consent:

  • From the App itself: in *Settings › Your account* you can export all your data (right of access and portability) and delete your account and all your data (right of erasure).
  • By email: writing to gjcentelles@proton.me. We will reply within one month at most.

Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription; to cancel it, go to *Settings › your name › Subscriptions* on your device.

If you believe we have not handled your rights properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch data protection authority, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), the authority of the country we operate from, or with the authority of your country of residence (in Spain, the AEPD, www.aepd.es).

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: encryption in transit and at rest, EU hosting, and row-level access control (each user can only access their own data). Your health data is not included in diagnostic logs or third-party analytics.

10. Minimum age

The App is intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal or service changes. We will publish the current version at this same address and indicate the date of the last update. If the changes affect the processing of your health data, we will inform you and, where appropriate, ask for your consent again.
